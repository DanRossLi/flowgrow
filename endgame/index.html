A finished island

    Flowgrow · Endgame

    A finished island: the result of a very good run, made by real play of the game's own rules. Drag to look around and pinch to zoom. Tap a tile to see what it is. A padlock is a locked tile.

    There is no limit on locks. Press and hold a tile on the island (or right-click it) to lock it: its neighbours, at its level or below, are copied to the back of your pile, and a locked tile can't grow any more.
